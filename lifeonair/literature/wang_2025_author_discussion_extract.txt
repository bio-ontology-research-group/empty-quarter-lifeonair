L1360: trophic microbes within the microbial communities. Previous

L1364: of energy- intensive transporters by utilizing broad- specificity

L1366: and high- affinity ATP- binding cassette (ABC) transporters and

L1368:
L1369: their low- cost lifestyle (Roller and Schmidt2015). In arid soils
L1370:
L1371: in which organic C is limited and insufficient to meet micro-
L1372:
L1373: bial energy demands, these microbes can exploit atmospheric

L1375: trace gases such as H 2 and CO—produced via hydrogeno-

L1377: trophic fermentation and photochemical CO generation (Berney

L1379: etal.2014; Moxley and Smith1998; Tarr etal.1995). These gases

L1384: conditions while releasing large amounts of energy (Greening

L1386: and Grinter2022). As such, these trace gases represent a cost-
L1387:
L1388: effective and efficient source of energy for oligotrophic microbes

L1390: to grow under C- constrained conditions in infertile environ-
L1391:
L1392: ments (Bay, Dong, etal.2021), which is why ETGG and MTGG

L1399: clined to utilize inorganic energy (Figure6d,e). Additionally, we

L1502: conditions (Greening and Grinter 2022). Microorganisms con-

L1547: organic substrate is assimilated into microbial biomass rather
L1548:
L1549: than respired as CO 2, which further accelerates the C accumula-
