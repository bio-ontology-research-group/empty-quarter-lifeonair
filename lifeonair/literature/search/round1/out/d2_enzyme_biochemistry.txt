SYNTHESIS:
This literature reveals critical nuances and caveats for the manuscript:

1. **Root-adjacent loss (H1):** Concluding that plant carbon does not drive group 1l loss because NDVI or gRodon poorly correlate risks overstatement. As [Kropp et al. (2026)](file:///home/leechuck/Documents/papers/empty-quarter-mags/lifeonair/literature/search) proved, catabolite repression of high-affinity hydrogenases is highly metabolite-specific (e.g., GylR-mediated repression by glycerol) rather than a coarse copiotrophy response. Furthermore, [de la Porte et al. (2024)](file:///home/leechuck/Documents/papers/empty-quarter-mags/lifeonair/literature/search) showed rhizosphere inputs can sustain gas-oxidizing persistence in oligotrophic soils, meaning root-adjacent depletion is not universal across edaphic contexts.

2. **Decoupling and Redundancy (H2):** Broader taxonomic spread and functional redundancy of form I CoxL relative to high-affinity hydrogenases is well established ([Latorre et al., 2026](file:///home/leechuck/Documents/papers/empty-quarter-mags/lifeonair/literature/search); [Wildbur et al., 2026](file:///home/leechuck/Documents/papers/empty-quarter-mags/lifeonair/literature/search)). Rather than claiming novelty for decoupling, frame it as community-level niche differentiation, noting that individual soil isolates can co-utilize both gases ([Tveit et al., 2019](file:///home/leechuck/Documents/papers/empty-quarter-mags/lifeonair/literature/search)).

3. **Space-for-Time Substitution (H3):** The apparent "failure" of space-for-time substitution between regional aridity and temporal expedition sampling is expected under recent ecological theory: [Knapp et al. (2026)](file:///home/leechuck/Documents/papers/empty-quarter-mags/lifeonair/literature/search) demonstrate that multi-millennial climatic aridity (which selects genomic capacity and evolutionary gene retention; [Peng et al., 2026](file:///home/leechuck/Documents/papers/empty-quarter-mags/lifeonair/literature/search)) is fundamentally decoupled from transient weather pulses. Frame this as evolutionary inertia of genomic traits versus dynamic physiological flux ([Wang et al., 2026](file:///home/leechuck/Documents/papers/empty-quarter-mags/lifeonair/literature/search)).
