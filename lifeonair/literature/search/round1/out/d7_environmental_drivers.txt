SYNTHESIS:
This literature indicates two crucial vulnerabilities and provides strong mechanistic reinforcement for the manuscript:

First, attributing spatial capacity trends to ERA5 MAP ($\rho = -0.51$) overstates certainty. Gridded precipitation evaluations across Saudi Arabia ([Wang 2025](file:///home/leechuck/Documents/papers/empty-quarter-mags/lifeonair/literature/search), [Nelli 2024](file:///home/leechuck/Documents/papers/empty-quarter-mags/lifeonair/literature/search)) reveal ERA5 has poor accuracy (median KGE 0.21) and severe local biases in the hyperarid Rub' al-Khali due to near-zero gauge coverage. Site-level MAP correlations must be framed cautiously or cross-checked against regional ML fusions (e.g., SaRa).

Second, the failure of space-for-time substitution between expeditions is biologically consistent with recent desert ecophysiology: [Imminger (2024)](file:///home/leechuck/Documents/papers/empty-quarter-mags/lifeonair/literature/search) and [Demergasso (2023)](file:///home/leechuck/Documents/papers/empty-quarter-mags/lifeonair/literature/search) show desert communities prioritize basal cellular repair over division following moisture pulses, explaining compositional stability (2% temporal variance). General ecological theory ([Perret 2024](file:///home/leechuck/Documents/papers/empty-quarter-mags/lifeonair/literature/search)) confirms spatial climate variation systematically decouples from temporal dynamics.

Finally, [Román (2026)](file:///home/leechuck/Documents/papers/empty-quarter-mags/lifeonair/literature/search) and [Horstmann (2024)](file:///home/leechuck/Documents/papers/empty-quarter-mags/lifeonair/literature/search) present direct methodological challenges: translational activity upon wetting decouples from metagenomic abundance, and hyperarid soils accumulate non-viable extracellular DNA. Claims must strictly emphasize *genomic capacity* rather than in situ atmospheric gas flux. Nevertheless, the study's scale (550 metagenomes across 60 Empty Quarter sites) provides unprecedented regional coverage beyond small-scale halophyte studies ([Khan 2022](file:///home/leechuck/Documents/papers/empty-quarter-mags/lifeonair/literature/search)).
