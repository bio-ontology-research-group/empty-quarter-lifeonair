SYNTHESIS:
An *ISME Journal* referee will challenge three core narratives. First, claiming to reject "space-for-time substitution" using a 31-month, 5-expedition dataset is overstated. Spatial aridity gradients represent multi-decadal steady-state community assembly, whereas short inter-expedition intervals merely sample transient weather fluctuations and pulse dynamics ([Damgaard 2019](file:///dev/null#L1-L1), [Schimel 2018](file:///dev/null#L1-L1)); rejecting substitution requires multi-decadal time-series ([Perret 2024](file:///dev/null#L1-L1)). Second, the 4.7-fold root-adjacent loss of group 1l risks dismissal as a passive taxonomic artefact: group 1l is phylogenetically restricted to oligotrophic phyla (e.g., Chloroflexota, Bacteroidota) that are systematically diluted by fast-growing copiotrophs in the rhizosphere ([Marasco 2018](file:///dev/null#L1-L1), [Bulgarelli 2013](file:///dev/null#L1-L1)). However, recent direct evidence for carbon catabolite repression of high-affinity hydrogenases ([Kropp 2026](file:///dev/null#L1-L1)) provides a defensible physiological mechanism that the authors should emphasize. Third, H2/CO decoupling and the broader taxonomic redundancy of Form I CoxL are already well-established macroecological traits ([King 2007](file:///dev/null#L1-L1), [Islam 2019](file:///dev/null#L1-L1)), so framing decoupling as a novel finding will trigger referee criticism. Finally, using gene copies per genome equivalent as a capacity proxy requires explicit caveats regarding dormancy and transcription decoupling ([Rocca 2015](file:///dev/null#L1-L1), [Blazewicz 2013](file:///dev/null#L1-L1)), and spatial autocorrelation must be explicitly controlled to substantiate aridity-driven selection over spatial confounding ([Dormann 2007](file:///dev/null#L1-L1)).
