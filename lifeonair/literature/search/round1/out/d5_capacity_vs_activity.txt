SYNTHESIS:
This literature underscores two critical caveats and refines two core interpretations for the manuscript:

1. **Activity vs. Capacity Overstatement:** In situ chamber fluxes in arid soils ([Buzzard et al., 2022](file:///home/leechuck/Documents/papers/empty-quarter-mags/lifeonair/literature/search)) and process models ([Bertagni et al., 2021](file:///home/leechuck/Documents/papers/empty-quarter-mags/lifeonair/literature/search)) demonstrate that hyperarid soils exhibit near-zero basal H₂ uptake despite high genomic potential, surging only after hydration. Framing group 1l abundance as active scavenging rather than survival potential under prolonged dormancy ([Schulze-Makuch et al., 2018](file:///home/leechuck/Documents/papers/empty-quarter-mags/lifeonair/literature/search); [Couradeau et al., 2019](file:///home/leechuck/Documents/papers/empty-quarter-mags/lifeonair/literature/search)) would be heavily challenged under ISME Journal scrutiny ([Rocca et al., 2015](file:///home/leechuck/Documents/papers/empty-quarter-mags/lifeonair/literature/search)).
2. **Relic DNA Confounder:** Hyperarid soils preserve extracellular DNA for years ([Horstmann et al., 2024](file:///home/leechuck/Documents/papers/empty-quarter-mags/lifeonair/literature/search); [Li et al., 2026](file:///home/leechuck/Documents/papers/empty-quarter-mags/lifeonair/literature/search)). The temporal invariance across expeditions (2% variance) may partially reflect relic DNA buffering against real-time turnover ([Carini et al., 2020](file:///home/leechuck/Documents/papers/empty-quarter-mags/lifeonair/literature/search)).
3. **Space-for-Time Disconnect is Expected:** Claiming novelty in the failure of space-for-time substitution across expeditions is overstated; ecological theory ([Damgaard, 2019](file:///home/leechuck/Documents/papers/empty-quarter-mags/lifeonair/literature/search)) and empirical qSIP ([Foley et al., 2023](file:///home/leechuck/Documents/papers/empty-quarter-mags/lifeonair/literature/search)) demonstrate that multi-decadal mean annual precipitation shapes community genomic traits independently of short-term weather fluctuations.
4. **H1 Mechanism:** [Baril & Constant (2023)](file:///home/leechuck/Documents/papers/empty-quarter-mags/lifeonair/literature/search) proved labile carbon directly represses high-affinity hydrogenases. Weak explanatory power from NDVI/copiotrophy suggests specific root exudate chemistry rather than bulk plant biomass mediates 1l exclusion.
