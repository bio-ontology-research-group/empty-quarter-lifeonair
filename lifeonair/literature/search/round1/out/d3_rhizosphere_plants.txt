SYNTHESIS:
Claiming that desert plants suppress trace-gas oxidizers broadly is not entirely novel: Li et al. (2023) already documented reduced trace-gas oxidizers beneath desert shrub canopies, attributing this to organic carbon favoring copiotrophs. The literature indicates that your manuscript's true novelty and impact lie in refuting that simple paradigm:

1. **Selective Decoupling Over Broad Loss:** Group 1l is specifically lost (4.7-fold), while 1h and form-I CoxL persist. Carboxydotrophy is phylogenetically flexible across versatile, root-competent taxa ([King 2002](file:///home/leechuck/Documents/papers/empty-quarter-mags/lifeonair/literature/search); [Xu 2025](file:///home/leechuck/Documents/papers/empty-quarter-mags/lifeonair/literature/search)), whereas group 1l represents an ultra-specialized oligotrophic survival trait.
2. **Failure of Carbon/Copiotrophy Proxies:** Global models ([Karbin 2024](file:///home/leechuck/Documents/papers/empty-quarter-mags/lifeonair/literature/search)) and life-history frameworks ([Yan 2025](file:///home/leechuck/Documents/papers/empty-quarter-mags/lifeonair/literature/search)) assume organic carbon and growth-rate potential drive trace-gas displacement. Finding that NDVI and gRodon explain little indicates that harsh sand-filtering and rhizosheath competition ([Marasco 2018, 2022](file:///home/leechuck/Documents/papers/empty-quarter-mags/lifeonair/literature/search); [de la Porte 2020](file:///home/leechuck/Documents/papers/empty-quarter-mags/lifeonair/literature/search)) dominate over soluble carbon priming.
3. **Capacity vs. Flux Boundaries:** [Piché-Choquette 2018](file:///home/leechuck/Documents/papers/empty-quarter-mags/lifeonair/literature/search) demonstrates that [NiFe]-hydrogenase gene abundances decouple from in situ fluxes. Frame results as genomic capacity buffered by millennia of hyperaridity: short-term rain pulses cause transient diffusive/physiological shifts ([Bertagni 2021](file:///home/leechuck/Documents/papers/empty-quarter-mags/lifeonair/literature/search)) without turning over stable community capacity ([Marasco 2022](file:///home/leechuck/Documents/papers/empty-quarter-mags/lifeonair/literature/search)), explaining why space-for-time substitution fails.
