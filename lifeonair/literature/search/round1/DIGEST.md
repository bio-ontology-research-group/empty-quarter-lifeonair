## Cross-direction themes raised by Flash (to be checked, not established)

1. **Compositional artefact for H1**: relative (per-genome) profiling can show oligotroph "loss" when rhizosphere biomass expands (Azarbad 2022); growth rate is reported as a dominant predictor of the rhizosphere effect (Lopez et al. 2023 ISME J), which bears on the claim that growth rate explains only a minor part of the 1l loss.
2. **Group 1l physiology**: Imminger 2024 (Nat Commun) reportedly finds desert 1l expression responsive to hydration; Kropp 2026 (mSystems) catabolite repression of high-affinity hydrogenase by glycerol; Baril & Constant 2023 labile-carbon repression; de la Porte 2024 rhizosphere effect promoting gas-oxidation persistence (contrary direction).
3. **Space-for-time**: failure of substitution is predicted by theory when temporal variance is small relative to the spatial gradient (Blois 2013, Damgaard 2019, Perret 2024, Lovell 2023, Knapp 2026 aridity vs drought); legacy effects (Hawkes 2017, Canarini 2021). Risk that "space-for-time fails" is framed as more novel/strong than a 31-month design permits.
4. **H2 novelty**: broader taxonomic spread/redundancy of form-I CoxL than of high-affinity hydrogenases reported elsewhere (King 2007, Islam 2019, Liu 2023 Dalangtan, Latorre 2026, Wildbur 2026).
5. **Covariate quality**: ERA5 precipitation reportedly poor over hyperarid Saudi Arabia (Wang 2025, Nelli 2024).
6. **Capacity vs activity / relic DNA**: Buzzard 2022, Bertagni 2021, Horstmann 2024, Li 2026 eLife, Carini 2020, Blazewicz 2013, Rocca 2015; contamination reporting standards for low-biomass samples (Fierer 2025 Nat Microbiol).
