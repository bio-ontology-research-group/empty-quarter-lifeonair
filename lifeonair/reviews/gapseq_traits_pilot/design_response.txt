Design review dispositions, 12 October 2026

1. Genus labels: already implemented as order + named genus, or order + family
for unassigned genera. Genus holdout is not family/order holdout; shared higher
taxonomy is deliberate and does not constitute genus leakage.
2. H2 transport: the audit logs H2-involving reactions, not verified hydrogenase
counts. H2 symport could be included. Primary traits contain no H2 and no
hydrogen-reaction deletion is used in this pilot. No phenotype change needed.
3. Site validation: accepted description that this is repeatability for
represented MAGs, not unseen-lineage prediction; this was explicit in the plan.
The baseline is fixed-effect ridge, not an estimated taxonomic random effect.
4. The review's claim that closing sinks enforces strict mass balance is not
adopted: mass/charge balance of internal reactions still needs separate evidence.
5. Independently fixed before field fitting: samples lacking CoverM measurements
are excluded, never imputed as all-zero abundance.
