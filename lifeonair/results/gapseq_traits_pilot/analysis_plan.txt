Gapseq-first predictive feasibility pilot — 12 October 2026
Specified before model phenotypes or their ecological associations are inspected.

Aim: test whether gapseq-derived metabolic traits improve prediction of relative
root-versus-bulk abundance responses. Gapsmith is not an input or validity gate.
Use existing gapseq reconstructions only when original input contig sequences
exactly match current representatives. Model availability determines scope.

Select at most 60 MAGs: 20 order-matched triplets of group-1l, group-1h and neither
controls. Require >=90% completeness, <=5% contamination, no R3 contamination
flags, no contig-subset IDs. Carrier groups exclude 1l/1h/2a co-carriage; neither
also excludes form-I CoxL. Use the previously frozen taxonomy/quality/marker
inventory but not gapsmith phenotypes. Prioritise within-genus/family matching
and genome quality, distribute triplets across all available orders before
adding a second/third triplet, and do not use abundance responses in selection.
Scope initially restricted to single-sample MAGs with traceable gapseq inputs.

Run draft and filled gapseq models. Keep original models unchanged. Close biomass
for ATP-maintenance tests; close non-exchange boundary reactions. Run closed,
inorganic, permissive-growth and native-medium controls. Separate solver failure
from optimal zero. Equal-organic-carbon panel of 12 substrates as in the earlier
pilot, oxygen cap 10. Test ATP maintenance and growth with common mineral/trace
metal medium. Growth failure is a model/media limitation unless positive controls
and biosynthetic requirements are reconciled. ATP features do not claim growth.

Primary predictor set (fixed, no feature search): ATP substrate breadth /12 and
log1p ATP production on a mixture of all 12 substrates at total 6 mmol organic
C/gDW/h (equal carbon per substrate). Run carbon 0.06/0.6/6 and oxygen 0.1/1/10
factorially as secondary diagnostics. H2 absent for primary traits: these tests
must not simply reproduce the marker used to define the groups. Also record
H2 benefit at zero organic carbon, but do not infer atmospheric affinity.

Field response: CoverM relative-abundance percent, restricted to current
high-quality read-screened samples (id_floor60, >=10 SCGs), root and depth-matched
bulk. Median biological replicates in site x expedition x compartment. For each
MAG and paired visit compute log2((root+epsilon)/(bulk+epsilon)), epsilon0.001
percentage points, with 0.0001 and0.01 sensitivities. Do not renormalise to the
selected MAG panel. Require detection >=0.01% in either compartment in >=5 paired
visits across >=3 sites, assessed without using response direction. Both-zero
visits are excluded from that MAG's fit. Treat the response as relative only.

Predict equal-site-weighted mean responses for each MAG. Baseline predictors:
log predicted doubling time (missingness indicator and training-only median
imputation), log genome length, completeness, contamination, marker group and
order. Use fixed ridge penalty10 with training-only continuous scaling and
categorical encoding; compare the baseline with baseline+the two metabolic
traits. No model/hyperparameter/feature selection based on pilot performance.

Validation A: five deterministic grouped folds by genus (unassigned genera are
kept together within their family/order). Train on all sites for training genera,
predict held-out genera's across-site response. Validation B: five grouped site
folds; fit MAG-level training-site means and predict test-site means. B measures
geographic repeatability for represented MAGs, not novel-lineage generalisation.
Require >=3 sites on each side for a MAG in B. Evaluate MSE improvement and fold
consistency; also report baseline-relative MAE. All splitting is group-aware.
Use 199 within-order shuffles of the paired metabolic trait vector to benchmark
improvement while preserving marker/quality/taxonomic baseline features.

Pragmatic scale-up rule (not a claim of statistical confirmation): >=80% filled
models pass energy QC and have positive ATP on at least one supplied carbon
source; >=30 usable MAGs, >=15 genus groups and >=6 orders in the field analysis;
>=5% lower cross-validated MSE in BOTH genus and site validation, improvement in
>=3/5 folds of each, and combined improvement above the90th percentile of the
within-order permutation null. Require positive improvement at both alternative
pseudocounts; report draft sensitivity rather than requiring agreement with an
incomplete draft. If criteria pass, extend to the remaining exact-match eligible
gapseq MAGs, freeze the pilot as discovery, and evaluate the added cohort with
pilot-selected specifications. Do not tune against the new cohort.

If criteria fail, finish the pilot, report which gate failed, and do not scale
automatically. A feasibility/representation failure is not a biological null.
This is exploratory work using an already-studied field dataset, not prospective
external validation. Traits are feasible capacities under assumed media.

Implementation clarification before outcomes: require all five validation folds
and at least30 distinct test MAGs in each validation scheme. The within-order
shuffle is a heuristic screening reference, not a formal conditional-independence
p-value. Taxonomic and quality covariates may correlate with metabolic traits.

Cohort correction after the initial diagnostic run: apply the paper's established
sites1-60 restriction (h123_v2.R line55). The initial implementation inadvertently
included the site64 paired visit. Preserve the initial output and script under
superseded_site_scope/. This is correction to the target study population, not
selection based on the sign or predictive gain. All analysis choices and gates
remain unchanged.
