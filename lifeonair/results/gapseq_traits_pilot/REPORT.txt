Gapseq-first metabolic-trait prediction pilot
12 October 2026

Decision

Do not scale up the tested two-trait prediction approach. The gapseq models
passed the energy/feasibility checks, but the metabolic traits did not improve
prediction across held-out genera. The small improvement across sites is worth
recording, but does not meet the prespecified requirement for improvement in
both validation schemes. Field coverage also fell below the planned minimum.
This decision is specific to these traits and this prediction task; it is not
an assessment that gapseq is unusable or that the biological hypothesis is false.
No new claims were added to the manuscript.

Design and coverage

Gapseq is the sole source of metabolic reconstructions. Neither gapsmith models
nor agreement with gapsmith was used as a validity condition. The shared numerical
helpers implement exchange bounds and ATP hydrolysis, not reconstruction.

Of 759 current, high-quality, unflagged single-sample candidate MAGs, 720 had
both gapseq reconstructions and exact agreement of original/current unordered
contig-sequence hashes. Thirty-nine lacked the required source/model files.
The exact-match pool includes 70 group-1l, 60 group-1h and 590 neither controls.
The frozen matching rules yielded 12 complete triplets: 36 genomes, 12 per group,
across nine orders. They were selected without inspecting field responses.
Although the target allowed up to 60 MAGs, no unmatched genomes were added to
inflate the panel. The panel contains 27 genus groups before field filtering.

Model scenarios and checks

We tested draft and filled gapseq models with 12 individual organic substrates
at equal organic-carbon supply, and a mixture with equal carbon contribution
from those substrates. The mixture was tested at carbon caps 0.06, 0.6 and 6
mmol C/gDW/h and oxygen caps 0.1, 1 and 10 mmol/gDW/h. These are hypothetical resource
constraints, not measured root exudates. H2 was absent from the primary traits.

The two prespecified predictors were substrate breadth for ATP maintenance
(number positive /12) and log 1p ATP production on the mixture at carbon 6/oxygen 10.
No additional traits, penalties or feature combinations were searched after
seeing prediction performance. Growth and H2 scenarios are secondary diagnostics.

All 3,312 scenario optimisations were optimal. All 72 draft/filled variants
passed the closed-exchange and inorganic-medium ATP tests. All 36 filled models
produced ATP on at least one carbon source and grew under permissive all-exchange
bounds. All 1,728 checks that optional carbon/oxygen must not lower the optimum
passed. These checks do not establish full thermodynamic or physiological validity.

Median filled-model ATP substrate breadth was 10/12 in all three groups.
Median mixture-supported ATP production was 10.81 (1l),10.64 (1h) and 8.12
(neither) mmol ATP/gDW/h, under the chosen uptake constraints. Draft breadth
medians were 7, 7 and 8. Drafts failed permissive growth; only one filled model
supported growth on any individually supplied substrate in the common defined
medium. These growth results are insufficient to infer actual auxotrophies or
substrate non-utilisation. ATP-feasibility traits were used as specified.

Field data and validation

The final field cohort uses the manuscript's established sites 1-60 restriction
and read-screening QC. It contains 430 root/bulk samples and 106 paired site-visits
across 47 sites. Its paired-visit identifiers exactly match the frozen main-paper
root/bulk contrast table. Replicates were aggregated by median. The selected
MAGs' CoverM percentages were not renormalised to the panel.

A MAG required detection >=0.01% in either compartment in at least 5 paired visits
across at least 3 sites. Twenty-six MAGs, spanning 23 genus groups and 9 orders,
passed this filter. Both-zero visits were excluded from the response. Responses
were log 2((root+epsilon)/(bulk+epsilon)), averaged within site and then equally
across sites. The primary epsilon was 0.001 percentage points; sensitivity values
were 0.0001 and 0.01. This measures relative representation, not absolute abundance.

The baseline used predicted doubling time, genome size, completeness,
contamination, marker group and order. The augmented model added only the two
metabolic traits. Ridge penalty 10 was fixed. All predictor imputation, scaling
and categorical encoding were fitted on each training set only.

Validation A held out whole genera in five folds, yielding 26 test predictions.
Validation B held out sites in five folds, yielding 97 predictions for 22 distinct
MAGs with sufficient training/test-site coverage. B tests repeatability across
sites for represented MAGs, not unseen-genome generalisation. Errors were
weighted so repeatedly evaluated MAGs did not gain extra total weight.

Final filled-model results at epsilon 0.001:
                         Baseline MSE   With traits MSE   Change in error
Held-out genera              2.371          2.547             7.44% higher
Held-out sites               3.349          3.240             3.25% lower

Only 1/5 genus folds improved; all 5/5 site folds improved. The small site-level
signal therefore has directional consistency, but did not reach the specified
5% improvement and did not generalise across genera.

Pseudocount sensitivity preserved the contrast. Genus prediction worsened by
8.51% at epsilon 0.0001 and 3.84% at 0.01; site prediction improved by 2.59% and 3.79%.
Draft models also failed the combined criterion: genus error increased 7.96%,
while site error decreased 0.56% at the primary pseudocount.

The mean improvement across the two validation schemes was -2.09%. For 199
within-order shuffles of the paired trait vector, the 90th-percentile improvement
was+0.24%; the observed result did not exceed this reference. The rank-tail
fraction was 0.295. This is a heuristic screening reference, not a formal p-value.

Scale-up criteria

Model quality/feasibility: PASS (36/36 filled models).
Field coverage >=30 MAGs, >=15 genus groups, >=6 orders: FAIL (26 MAGs / 23 genus groups / 9 orders).
At least 30 distinct evaluated MAGs in each validation: FAIL (26 and 22).
At least 5% MSE improvement in both validations: FAIL.
Improvement in at least 3/5 folds of each validation: FAIL (1/5 and 5/5).
Combined gain above the shuffle 90th percentile: FAIL.
Positive gain in both validations at both alternative pseudocounts: FAIL.

Consequently, the larger 720-model eligible set was not simulated for this task.

Interpretation and limits

The pilot successfully establishes exact-genome model coverage and a functioning
gapseq-only workflow. These broad carbon-energy traits do not currently provide
additional support for the paper's root-associated lineage-turnover hypothesis.
The sample is small and purposively matched, and the usable field cohort is
smaller still. Poor generalisation does not rule out metabolic effects; it means
this particular extension has not earned a larger analysis or a manuscript claim.

The test did not evaluate a curated hydrogen-specific carbon-sparing mechanism,
hydrogenase regulation/affinity, experimentally measured exudates, or community
competition. Those are separate hypotheses. Standard FBA resource capacities
alone cannot establish those mechanisms. No causal mediation is claimed.

Audit and reproducibility

The first field implementation inadvertently included the paired visit at site 64.
This was detected by comparing cohort counts with h123_v2.R; the final rerun
applied the existing paper boundary. Initial outputs and source are retained in
superseded_site_scope/. Predictors, penalty, validation algorithm and gates were
not changed. Job 53639517 is superseded; job 53640046 produced the final fits.

Gemini Pro through agy reviewed the design/simulation and prediction code.
Accepted corrections and limitations are in ../../reviews/gapseq_traits_pilot/.
In particular, optimal-zero traits remain eligible; the cohort's positive-ATP
requirement is separate from individual solver validity.

The frozen-output verifier checks exact genome matching, paired-visit identity,
scenario closure, optional-resource monotonicity and independently recalculates
all 72 reported prediction metrics from the saved predictions. All checks pass.
The existing paper verification also passes 364 checks. The repository-wide
harness retains its 30 pre-existing P4 claim/prose failures; its build succeeds.
