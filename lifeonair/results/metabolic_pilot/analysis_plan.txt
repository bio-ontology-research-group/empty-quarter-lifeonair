Metabolic-model pilot, specified before inspecting simulation phenotypes
12 October 2026

Question: do model-derived resource-use capabilities distinguish group-1l carriers
from group-1h carriers and non-carriers, and help explain their relative depletion
beside roots? This is an exploratory extension of the existing field study.

Source: exact-ID gapsmith draft/filled models and added-reaction ledgers under
06_darkmatter/gapsmith_models on IBEX. Verify current representative correspondence
and protein provenance. No fuzzy stripping of single/self bin identifiers. Exclude
contig-subset representatives from this initial pilot, plus the round-3 contamination
union. Require >=90% completeness and <=5% contamination. Carrier groups exclude
1l/1h/2a co-carriage; non-carriers lack all three and form-I CoxL. Record form-I
co-carriage in the two hydrogenase groups.

Choose non-reused triplets within named orders, prioritising matching genus/family
and genome quality. At most three triplets per order and 24 overall. Choose before
using model phenotypes or field abundance. These are taxonomically matched pilot
models, not a random catalogue sample or an independently replicated set of species.

Audit model exchanges, biomass objective, hydrogen stoichiometry/GPRs, gap-fill
provenance and ATP maintenance. Evaluate draft and filled models separately. Close
non-exchange boundary uptake and reverse biomass; close biomass during maintenance
tests. Maximise ATP hydrolysis with explicit water/proton bookkeeping. Test all-uptake-
closed and mineral/oxygen-only energy controls, plus hydrogen deletion and oxygen
dependence. A model generating energy without a supplied donor is ineligible for
ecological interpretation until repaired. Record infeasible/unbounded/failed LPs
separately from optimal zero predictions. Check positive controls before interpreting
absent substrate growth or zero hydrogen benefit as biological absence.

Screen a small panel of sugars, organic acids and amino acids with equal carbon
input and explicit mineral/oxygen bounds. Use both biomass feasibility and ATP
maintenance to distinguish biomass-network gaps from absent energy metabolism.
Test carbon supply and hydrogen supply factorially; hydrogen is optional uptake,
so allowing it must not reduce the optimum. Report feasible capacity under assumed
flux bounds, without assigning atmospheric concentrations directly to uptake fluxes.
Quantify dependence on draft vs gap-filled reactions and supported transport.

Go/no-go: expand and integrate with the field data only if model QC passes, the
relevant respiratory/transport reactions have defensible genomic support, and the
pilot yields interpretable variation. All-negative models trigger an audit of
positive controls and pathway representation, not a conclusion about inactivity.
Do not force the desired phenotype by gap-filling in hydrogen media.

If adequate, test model traits against paired root/bulk abundance changes with
taxonomic blocking and genome-quality/growth covariates. Use site and lineage
holdouts where sample size permits; do not call coefficient attenuation causal
mediation. A small pilot alone will not support a fitted high-dimensional field model.

Independent design review: ../../reviews/metabolic_pilot/design_review.txt. Its
useful suggestions on controls and provenance are adopted. Its assertions that
gap filling necessarily erases variation, >80% zero hydrogen benefit proves model
failure, and attenuation proves explanation are not adopted: these require evidence.

Compute: IBEX CPU Slurm, workspace 06_atmospheric/metabolic_pilot_clean. No model
reconstruction or modification of the source models. Record scripts, input hashes,
solver versions, status codes and job IDs. Keep main manuscript conclusions unchanged
until the pilot is evaluated.

Smoke-test refinement before the final pilot: growth assays additionally supply
Mn, Zn, Cu, Co, Ni, Fe(II) and Fe(III), each at 0.01 mmol/gDW/h, to avoid mechanically
blocking metal-containing biomass. No organic supplements are added. The original
models' predicted growth media include numerous amino acids/vitamins; a lack of
minimal-medium growth can therefore reflect their reconstructed auxotrophies.
ATP maintenance uses the original mineral medium without these trace additions.

Secondary diagnostic: compare available gapseq reconstructions for the pilot's
single-sample bins. Require exact unordered contig-sequence hashes between their
original input genomes and current representatives before any model comparison.
Run the same ATP-maintenance conservation controls, hydrogen scenario and carbon
panel. Treat differences as reconstruction sensitivity; do not equate shared
reaction names with experimentally validated enzyme specificity.
